This Privacy Policy explains how JART Software Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your personal information. Please read it carefully. By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described herein.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you submit our contact or inquiry form, we collect:

  • Identity data: First name, last name
  • Contact data: Email address
  • Professional data: Company or organization name (optional)
  • Service preference data: Service(s) of interest, estimated budget range
  • Communication data: Your message and project description

When you engage us for services under a formal contract, we may additionally collect billing information, project documentation, and technical access credentials necessary to deliver the contracted services.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, our hosting infrastructure may automatically record:

  • IP address (anonymized where technically feasible)
  • Browser type, version, and language settings
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed and time spent on pages
  • Referring URL
  • Date and time of access

This information is used solely for security monitoring, fraud prevention, and aggregate statistical analysis. We do not build individual profiles from this data.

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use a limited set of cookies. Please refer to our Cookie Policy for full details, including a cookie table, types of cookies used, and instructions for managing your preferences.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For individuals located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases pursuant to Article 6 of the GDPR:

Processing Activity Lawful Basis GDPR Article
Responding to contact form inquiries Legitimate interests — responding to a business inquiry you initiated Art. 6(1)(f)
Delivering contracted services Performance of a contract Art. 6(1)(b)
Compliance with legal obligations (e.g., tax, accounting records) Legal obligation Art. 6(1)(c)
Service analytics and security monitoring Legitimate interests — ensuring website security and improving our services Art. 6(1)(f)
Marketing communications (only if separately requested) Consent Art. 6(1)(a)

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have conducted a balancing test and determined that our interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms, given that the data is limited to professional inquiry data provided voluntarily and is processed for the expected purpose of the contact.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide information about our services
  • To assess whether we can meet your requirements and prepare proposals or quotes
  • To onboard you as a client and deliver contracted software or consulting services
  • To send project updates, milestone notifications, and deliverables
  • To issue invoices and process payments
  • To maintain our client relationship and provide post-engagement support
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
  • To protect our website from unauthorized access, abuse, or fraud
  • To improve our website and service offerings using aggregated, anonymized insights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for automated individual decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Service Providers (Data Processors)

We engage the following categories of third-party service providers who process data on our behalf under written data processing agreements:

  • Form processing: FormSubmit.co — processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our designated email address. FormSubmit acts as a data processor under our instruction.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — hosts our static website files. GitHub's servers may process IP addresses and access logs. GitHub, Inc. is certified under EU-US Data Privacy Framework mechanisms.
  • Email communication: Our internal email provider — used to receive and respond to inquiries.
  • Project management and delivery tools: Standard enterprise SaaS tools used during active engagements (disclosed per-client in the relevant services agreement).

5.2 Disclosure Required by Law

We may disclose your personal information when required to do so by law, court order, or binding government authority, or where disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights, prevent fraud, or ensure the safety of persons.

5.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified via prominent notice on our website or by direct communication prior to your data becoming subject to a materially different privacy policy.

7. International Data Transfers

Our services are operated from the United States. If you are located outside the United States, including in the EEA or United Kingdom, please be aware that your personal information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, which may not offer equivalent data protection laws to your home jurisdiction.

Where required by applicable law, we rely on the following transfer mechanisms to lawfully transfer data internationally:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — European Commission-approved clauses (2021/914) incorporated into contracts with our data processors.
  • EU-US and UK Extension Data Privacy Framework — for processors certified under the DPF program.
  • Adequacy decisions — where the destination country has received an adequacy decision from the European Commission.

You may request a copy of the applicable transfer safeguards by contacting us at privacy@jartsoftwaresolutions.com.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR — EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the GDPR and equivalent national legislation:

Article 15

Right of Access

Obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy of it, along with information about how it is processed.

Article 16

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data we hold about you.

Article 17

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data where there is no compelling reason for its continued processing ("right to be forgotten"), subject to our legal retention obligations.

Article 18

Right to Restriction

Request that we restrict processing of your data while a dispute about its accuracy or our legal basis is resolved.

Article 20

Right to Portability

Receive the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (where processing is based on consent or contract).

Article 21

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

Article 7(3)

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Article 77

Right to Lodge a Complaint

Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe we have not handled your data lawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@jartsoftwaresolutions.com. We will respond within 30 days (extendable by a further two months for complex requests). We may require identity verification before processing your request.

9. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020):

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months, the sources of that information, the purposes for which it was collected, and the categories of third parties with whom it was shared.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out mechanism is required, but you may contact us to confirm our practices.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: We do not collect or use sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide our services.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at privacy@jartsoftwaresolutions.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days of receipt. Verification of your identity will be required.

10. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the risk level of our data processing activities. These include:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • Access controls based on the principle of least privilege
  • Multi-factor authentication for internal systems
  • Regular security assessments and code reviews
  • Vendor due diligence and data processing agreements with all sub-processors

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected individuals without undue delay and will report to the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by GDPR Article 33.

For full details of our security practices, see our Security Policy.
