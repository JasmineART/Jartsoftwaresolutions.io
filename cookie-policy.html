This Cookie Policy explains how JART Software Solutions uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on jartsoftwaresolutions.com. It is intended to be transparent and easy to understand. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site operator.

Beyond cookies, websites may also use related technologies such as:

  • Local Storage / Session Storage: Web storage APIs that store data in your browser, persisting between sessions (local storage) or cleared when the browser tab closes (session storage)
  • Pixel tags / web beacons: Tiny transparent images embedded in pages or emails that trigger a request to a server when loaded, allowing activity tracking
  • Browser fingerprinting: Identifying a device by the combination of its technical characteristics

References to "cookies" in this policy include all such similar technologies unless otherwise stated.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions correctly and securely (strictly necessary)
  • Remember your preferences so you do not have to re-enter them (functional)
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it (analytics, where consented)
  • Protect our website against spam and abuse (security)

We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising, behavioural tracking for ad targeting, or to build profiles for sale to data brokers.

6. Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts (Inter and Syne typefaces), loaded directly from Google's CDN via fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.

When your browser loads a page, it sends a request to Google's servers to download the font files. This request includes your IP address and HTTP headers (browser type, referring URL). According to Google's documentation, Google Fonts does not set cookies and does not store personal data on Google Fonts API requests beyond standard server log data, which is deleted after a short period.

The basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing a consistent, typographically high-quality website experience. If you prefer that your IP address not be transmitted to Google, you may:

  • Use a browser extension that blocks Google Fonts requests
  • Configure your browser to use a font substitution that overrides web fonts

See Google Fonts Privacy FAQ for the most current information on their data practices.

9. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal that you can enable to request that websites do not track your browsing activity. There is currently no legally binding requirement to honour DNT signals, and industry standards for DNT have not been finalized.

JART Software Solutions respects the spirit of DNT. Given that we do not currently use third-party tracking or analytics cookies, DNT signals have no effect on our current processing. If we introduce analytics or tracking technologies in the future, we will evaluate how to honour DNT signals in compliance with applicable law and best practices at that time.

For California residents, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the CPRA provide stronger rights. Please see our Privacy Policy for CCPA-specific information, including how to submit an opt-out of sale/sharing request.
