    Discovery & Strategy

    We start by deeply understanding your business, goals, users, and constraints. This phase produces a detailed technical specification and clear project roadmap with defined milestones.

    Design & Architecture

    Our designers and architects craft the solution blueprint — from system architecture and database design to UI mockups, component libraries, and user flows. You approve before we write a line of code.
