This GDPR Statement is provided pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and supplements our Privacy Policy. It applies to all individuals located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland whose personal data is processed by JART Software Solutions.

1. Introduction

JART Software Solutions is committed to full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the UK GDPR (as retained in UK law by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018). We take data protection seriously and have implemented technical and organizational measures designed to protect the rights and freedoms of individuals whose personal data we process.

This statement is designed to ensure that you receive the information required under GDPR at the time your data is collected or, where data is obtained from other sources, within a reasonable time. It is a living document that we review and update regularly.

2. Data Controller Information

Under GDPR, JART Software Solutions acts as the data controller for personal data collected through our website and in connection with pre-contractual and contractual relationships with clients and prospective clients.

Where we process personal data on behalf of a client pursuant to a service agreement, we act as a data processor and that client is the data controller. In such cases, the processing is governed by the data processing agreement (DPA) between JART and the client.

5. Purposes of Processing

We process personal data for the following specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes (GDPR Art. 5(1)(b)):

  1. Responding to and managing pre-sales inquiries and client onboarding
  2. Delivering software development, consulting, and AI services under contract
  3. Managing client relationships and project communications
  4. Issuing invoices and managing accounts receivable
  5. Complying with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations
  6. Protecting our website and internal systems from unauthorized access and fraud
  7. Improving our services and website through aggregated, privacy-preserving analysis
  8. Sending service-related communications (project updates, security notices)
  9. Sending marketing communications where you have given explicit consent

We do not process personal data for purposes incompatible with those for which it was originally collected, unless we obtain your consent or are required to by law (compatibility test per GDPR Art. 6(4)).

6. Recipients and Sub-Processors

Personal data may be shared with the following categories of recipients, all of whom process data under appropriate contractual safeguards:

  • Form processing services — FormSubmit.co (routes contact form submissions to our email)
  • Website hosting — GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages hosting; EU-US DPF certified)
  • Email service provider — Our business email provider for receiving and responding to inquiries
  • Professional advisors — Legal, accounting, and auditing firms under contractual confidentiality
  • Regulators and authorities — Where required by applicable law
  • Project sub-contractors — Carefully selected specialists engaged on specific client projects, under DPA and confidentiality agreements

We do not sell personal data to any third party. We do not share personal data with advertising platforms or data brokers.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations. Retention periods are governed by our Data Retention Schedule and the principle of storage limitation (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e)):

  • Pre-contract inquiry data: 3 years from last contact (statute of limitations for contractual claims)
  • Client project data and communications: 7 years from project completion (accounting and legal obligations)
  • Financial and tax records: 7 years (applicable tax authority requirements)
  • Server and security logs: 90 days (rolling retention for security monitoring)
  • Consent records: For the duration of the relationship plus 5 years (to demonstrate compliance)

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be attributed to an individual. Deletion requests made under the right to erasure are processed within 30 days, subject to any overriding legal retention obligation.

9. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

We conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) pursuant to GDPR Article 35 for processing activities that are likely to result in a high risk to individuals' rights and freedoms. DPIA triggers include:

  • Large-scale processing of special category data
  • Systematic monitoring of publicly accessible areas
  • Novel use of AI or machine learning systems that process personal data
  • Processing involving vulnerable populations

All client AI projects involving the processing of personal data are evaluated for DPIA necessity prior to implementation. Where a DPIA indicates a high residual risk that cannot be mitigated, we consult with the competent supervisory authority prior to processing (Art. 36 prior consultation).

10. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

Pursuant to GDPR Article 22, we are required to inform you that:

JART Software Solutions does not subject individuals to decisions based solely on automated processing — including profiling — that produce legal or similarly significant effects on them in connection with our own operations (website inquiries, client management).

For client AI projects that implement automated decision-making features affecting end users, we advise clients of their obligations under Art. 22 and build in the required safeguards (human review mechanisms, right to contest, transparency notices) as part of the technical implementation.

11. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject, you have the following rights under GDPR, which we are obligated to fulfil:

Art. 13 / 14

Right to Be Informed

Receive clear, transparent information about how your data is processed. This GDPR Statement and our Privacy Policy fulfil this obligation.

Art. 15

Right of Access

Obtain confirmation of whether we process your data, access to a copy of that data, and supplementary information about the processing (purposes, categories, recipients, retention periods).

Art. 16

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete personal data without undue delay.

Art. 17

Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")

Request deletion of your personal data where: it is no longer necessary for its original purpose; you withdraw consent; you successfully object; or it was unlawfully processed. Subject to legal retention obligations.

Art. 18

Right to Restriction of Processing

Request that we restrict processing (i.e., store data but not process it further) pending the resolution of accuracy disputes, legal claims, or objections to processing.

Art. 19

Right to Notification

Where you have exercised rectification, erasure, or restriction, we will notify each recipient to whom your data was disclosed, unless this is impossible or involves disproportionate effort.

Art. 20

Right to Data Portability

Receive the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV) and, where technically feasible, request direct transmission to another controller. Applies where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means.

Art. 21

Right to Object

Object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) or for direct marketing purposes. We will cease processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or for the establishment of legal claims.

Art. 7(3)

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent.

Art. 77

Right to Lodge a Complaint

Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority if you believe we have not processed your data in accordance with the GDPR. See Section 13 for supervisory authority details.

12. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights described in Section 11, please submit a written request to:

Data Subject Rights Requests

Email: gdpr@jartsoftwaresolutions.com

Subject line: "GDPR Request — [Right you are exercising] — [Your Name]"

Include: Your full name, email address(es) used in dealings with us, a description of your request, and a form of identity verification (a copy of a government-issued ID or equivalent may be required for access or portability requests).

Response Timelines

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 5 business days
  • We will fulfil your request within one calendar month of receipt (Art. 12(3))
  • For complex or numerous requests, we may extend this by a further two months; we will inform you of any extension and the reasons within one month of receipt
  • Where we cannot take action on your request, we will explain the reasons within one month
  • We do not charge a fee for exercising your rights unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive (Art. 12(5))
