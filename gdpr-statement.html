This California Privacy Rights notice is provided pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (CPRA), California Civil Code §§ 1798.100–1798.199. It supplements our Privacy Policy and applies to California residents.

1. Overview

JART Software Solutions is a boutique software development firm headquartered in Sacramento, California. As a California business, we are committed to respecting the privacy rights of California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). This notice explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the rights California residents have over their personal information.

This notice applies to visitors to our website (jartsoftwaresolutions.com) and prospective and current clients who are California residents.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following business and commercial purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and provide information about our services
  • To assess project requirements and prepare proposals
  • To deliver software development, consulting, and AI services under contract
  • To manage client relationships and project communications
  • To issue invoices and process payments
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations (tax, accounting, contractual)
  • To protect our website and systems from unauthorized access, abuse, or fraud
  • To improve our website and services using aggregated, anonymized insights

We do not use personal information for targeted advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or automated individual decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. In the preceding 12 months, we have not sold or shared personal information for advertising purposes.

We disclose personal information only to the following categories of service providers who process data on our behalf to support our business operations:

  • Form processing: FormSubmit.co — routes contact form submissions to our email inbox
  • Website hosting: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) — hosts our static website files
  • Email provider: Our business email service for receiving and responding to inquiries
  • Professional advisors: Legal, accounting, and auditing firms under confidentiality agreements

We may also disclose personal information when required by law, court order, or binding government authority.

6. Your CCPA/CPRA Rights

As a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA and CPRA (California Civil Code § 1798.100 et seq.):

§ 1798.110

Right to Know

Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom it was shared.

§ 1798.105

Right to Delete

Request deletion of personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., completing a transaction, legal obligations, security purposes).

§ 1798.106

Right to Correct

Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you, taking into account the nature of the information and purposes of processing.

§ 1798.120

Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing

Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell or share personal information, so this right is not applicable to our current practices.

§ 1798.121

Right to Limit Sensitive PI Use

Limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information to what is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. We do not collect sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide our services.

§ 1798.125

Right to Non-Discrimination

Exercise your CCPA rights without being denied goods or services, charged different prices, provided a different level of service quality, or otherwise discriminated against.

7. How to Exercise Your Rights

To submit a verifiable California consumer rights request, contact us using the information below. We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days of receipt. If we require additional time (up to 90 days), we will notify you of the extension and the reason within the initial 45-day period.

Submit a Privacy Request

Email: Help@jartsoftwaresolutions.com

Subject line: "CCPA Request — [Right you are exercising] — [Your Name]"

Include: Your full name, email address used in dealings with us, a description of your request, and sufficient information for us to verify your identity.

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request to prevent unauthorized access to or disclosure of your personal information. We will not require you to create an account to submit a request.

You may also designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. Authorized agent requests require written permission or a power of attorney, and we may require direct verification from you to confirm the authorization.

8. Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights. We will not deny you goods or services, charge you different prices, provide a different quality of service, or suggest you will receive a different price or level of service as a result of exercising your rights.
